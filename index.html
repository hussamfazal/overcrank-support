Free vs Pro
Record, modes, live status, complications, and Siri are free forever. Pro ($9.99, one-time, family-shareable) adds full settings, presets, live monitor, media tools, multi-cam, and Adventure Mode.
Privacy
No analytics, no accounts, no servers. Camera communication is device-to-camera only, and Health data never leaves your device.

COMPATIBILITY & QUESTIONS

Know before you mount.

Built for MISSION 1 Pro. Compatible with HERO9 Black and later through the Open GoPro API.

MISSION 1 PROMISSION 1HERO9 BLACK +
Which GoPro cameras work with Overcrank?

Overcrank is built for GoPro MISSION 1 Pro and supports HERO cameras that use the Open GoPro API, including HERO9 Black and later. Exact settings depend on your camera and firmware.

Does the Apple Watch need the iPhone nearby?

For core Watch controls, Overcrank connects directly to the camera over Bluetooth. iPhone-powered features such as Live Monitor and framing preview use the phone.

What is included for free?

Record control, mode switching, live status, complications, and Siri or Action button recording are free. Pro adds full settings, presets, Live Monitor, media tools, Multi-Cam, and Adventure Mode.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Overcrank Pro is a one-time $9.99 purchase and supports Family Sharing.

What you just did

bluetooth_connected

Straight to the camera

Apple Watch talks to the MISSION 1 Pro over Bluetooth. Record, modes and settings don't need your iPhone in the loop.

sync_alt

It works both ways

Press the shutter on the camera and the watch knows. Battery, card time and the record timer stream back to the wrist.

rule

The camera's own rules

Ask for 120 fps at 8K and the camera steps down to 4K. Overcrank shows that the moment it happens, so nothing changes behind your back.

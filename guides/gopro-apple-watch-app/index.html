Questions

Is there a GoPro HERO13 Apple Watch app?

Not from GoPro. goWatch and Action Control name the HERO13 Black in their App Store listings. Overcrank is built for it, but not yet tested on one.

Does GoPro Quik have an Apple Watch app?

No. The GoPro Quik App Store listing covers iPhone and iPad only (checked 26 September 2026).

What happened to GoPro's Apple Watch app?

GoPro removed it, and owners still ask for it back on GoPro's own forum. See the thread.

Can Apple's Camera Remote control a GoPro?

No. Camera Remote on Apple Watch controls your iPhone's camera, not a GoPro.

Do they work on Apple Watch Ultra?

Overcrank needs watchOS 10 or later, which every Apple Watch Ultra runs. The other listings don't mention Ultra.

How do I connect a GoPro to Apple Watch?

Pair once on the camera, then tap it in the app on your watch. The 3 steps, with photos.

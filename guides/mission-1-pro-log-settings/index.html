GoPro MISSION 1 Pro Log settings

Four settings, in this order. Log looks flat out of the camera and gives you the most room to grade.

  • 10-bit
  • Max bit rate
  • GP-Log2
  • Fixed white balance

The four settings, in order

  1. 1

    Bit Depth

    10-bit

    Over a billion colours, so grades don't band. Set it first.

  2. 2

    Bit Rate

    Max

    The least compression, so more detail survives the grade.

  3. 3

    Color

    GP-Log2

    Flat on purpose: it keeps the most dynamic range for grading.

  4. 4

    White Balance

    4500K

    Any fixed value that matches your light. Auto drifts between shots.

Why 10-bit goes first

You set Bit Depth: 8-bit

Camera also changed

  • Bit Rate Max → High
  • Color GP-Log2 → Natural

Max and GP-Log2 need 10-bit. Going back to 10-bit didn't bring them back, so set 10-bit before the other three. Four more like this

Know before you shoot Log

  • Light

    Not for low light

    GoPro limits ISO to 400 in GP-Log2.

  • Editing

    It needs a grade

    It looks washed out until you apply a conversion or grade it.

  • Storage

    Bigger files

    Max bit rate fills cards faster. Check recording time left.

Questions

What is GP-Log2?

GoPro's log colour profile. It keeps more dynamic range, which helps in bright scenes, and looks washed out until you grade it.

Why did my GP-Log2 setting switch back to Natural?

On our camera, switching Bit Depth to 8-bit changed it, and Bit Rate dropped from Max to High. Set 10-bit, then set both again.

What white balance should I use for Log?

A fixed value that matches your light, not Auto. We used 4500K indoors; the camera offers 2300K to 6500K.

Can you shoot GP-Log2 in low light?

Not well. GoPro limits ISO to 400 in Log and doesn't recommend it for darker scenes.

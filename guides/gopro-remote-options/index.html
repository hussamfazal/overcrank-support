Questions

Does GoPro make a remote control?

Two: The Remote (US $84.99), with a screen and control of up to 5 cameras, and the one-button Waterproof Shutter Remote (US $59.99). Prices from gopro.com, 28 September 2026.

Which GoPro remote works with MISSION 1 Pro?

Both. GoPro lists the MISSION 1 series and HERO13 Black as compatible with The Remote and the Waterproof Shutter Remote.

Can I use my phone as a GoPro remote?

Yes, with GoPro's free Quik app. It works while the phone is in your hand, which is the catch when both hands are busy.

Can I start a GoPro recording with my voice?

Yes. MISSION 1 series cameras take voice commands to start and stop recording, take photos and change modes. It needs the camera to hear you. GoPro's command list.

Is there a GoPro remote for Apple Watch?

Not from GoPro. Third-party apps like Overcrank put record, modes and status on the watch. All four compared.

Best settings, by situation

What are the best MISSION 1 Pro settings?

For everyday video: 4K, 30 fps, Linear lens, HyperSmooth Auto Boost, Natural colour, and Auto for shutter, ISO and white balance. Change one thing at a time from there.

What are the best MISSION 1 Pro slow motion settings?

Raise the frame rate: 120 or 240 fps at 4K 16:9, or up to 480 fps at 1080p or 1440p. GoPro says 240 fps still plays smoothly slowed down 8 times.

What are the best MISSION 1 Pro cinematic settings?

24 or 30 fps, a fixed shutter at about double the frame rate, a fixed white balance, and Cinematic colour, or GP-Log2 if you'll grade.

What are the best MISSION 1 Pro low light settings?

Use the Low Light mode or 24 to 30 fps, and avoid GP-Log2: GoPro limits ISO to 400 in Log and doesn't recommend it for darker scenes.

Frame and motion

Resolution

How many pixels are recorded. More means more detail, but a hotter camera, shorter battery life and bigger files.

  • 8K
  • 4K (suggested)
  • 1440p
  • 1080p

Start with 4K. Plenty of detail without 8K's heat and file sizes. Pick 8K when you'll crop in the edit.

Aspect ratio

The shape of the frame.

  • 16:9 (suggested)
  • 4:3
  • 9:16

Start with 16:9. For YouTube and TV. 4:3 gives a taller frame to reframe later; 9:16 is for Shorts and Reels.

Frame rate

Frames per second. More frames mean smoother motion and slower slow motion.

  • 24
  • 30 (suggested)
  • 60
  • 120
  • 240
  • 480

Start with 30. Normal playback. Use 60 for fast action and 120 or more to slow it down. The fastest rates need lower resolutions.

Digital lens

How wide the view is.

  • SuperView
  • Wide
  • Linear (suggested)
  • Linear + Horizon Lock

Start with Linear. Wide, without the fisheye curve. Wide is the classic GoPro look. Horizon Lock needs Linear with HyperSmooth on.

HyperSmooth

Stabilisation. It crops the image slightly to keep it steady.

  • Off
  • Low
  • Auto Boost (suggested)

Start with Auto Boost. It crops only when it needs to. Turn it off on a tripod. It can change your frame rate: see how.

Motion blur

How much blur moving things keep.

  • Max Blur Reduction
  • Adaptive (suggested)
  • Cinematic Low
  • Cinematic Medium
  • Cinematic High

Start with Adaptive. Let the camera decide. The Cinematic options keep more blur for a filmic look.

Picture quality

Image tuning

Processing tuned for the scene.

  • Balanced (suggested)
  • Sport
  • Dive
  • Face

Start with Balanced. For everyday shots. Sport for fast movement, Dive underwater, Face for talking to camera.

Bit depth

How many colours are recorded: over 16 million at 8-bit, over a billion at 10-bit.

  • 8-bit (suggested)
  • 10-bit

Start with 8-bit. GoPro says 10-bit is only worth it if you'll colour grade.

Bit rate

How much data is recorded each second. More means less compression and bigger files.

  • Standard
  • High (suggested)
  • Max

Start with High. Max if you'll grade the footage.

Color

The colour profile.

  • Vibrant
  • Natural (suggested)
  • Cinematic
  • Flat
  • GP-Log2

Start with Natural. Realistic colour. Vibrant adds pop with no editing. Flat and GP-Log2 are for grading: Log settings.

HLG HDR

HDR video for scenes with very dark and very bright areas.

  • Off (suggested)
  • On

Start with Off. GoPro says it's meant for watching on HDR displays.

Sharpness

How crisp or soft the video looks.

  • Low
  • Medium (suggested)
  • High

Start with Medium. Low leaves more room if you'll edit.

Exposure and colour temperature

Shutter

How long each frame is exposed. Longer is brighter and blurrier.

  • Auto (suggested)
  • Fixed
  • Range

Start with Auto. Fixed at about double your frame rate (1/60 at 30 fps) gives natural motion blur; in daylight that needs an ND filter.

EV Comp

Makes the whole picture brighter or darker than the camera would choose.

  • −1.0
  • −0.5
  • 0.0 (suggested)
  • +0.5
  • +1.0

Start with 0.0. Try −0.5 to keep bright skies from blowing out. Range: −2.0 to +2.0.

White balance

How warm or cool colours look.

  • Auto (suggested)
  • 3200K
  • 4500K
  • 5500K
  • 6500K
  • Native

Start with Auto. Fix a value (2300K to 6500K) when shooting Log or matching cameras.

ISO

Brightens the image electronically, adding noise as it goes up.

  • Auto (suggested)
  • Fixed
  • Range

Start with Auto. Use Range to cap the noise; GoPro says to keep ISO as low as you can.

Descriptions follow GoPro's settings article; which options you get depends on resolution and frame rate (see GoPro's MISSION 1 PRO table). White balance and EV Comp show a selection of their values.

Can Apple Watch Camera Remote control a GoPro?

No. Camera Remote is a viewfinder and shutter for your iPhone's camera, and it can't see a GoPro. To control a GoPro from the watch you need a GoPro app on it.

Camera Remote vs a GoPro app on Apple Watch

Camera Remote (Apple)

  • A live viewfinder of your iPhone's camera
  • Takes photos and records video on the iPhone
  • Flash, a shutter timer and a review of your shots
  • Works within Bluetooth range of the iPhone, about 10 m (33 ft)

A GoPro app (Overcrank)

  • Talks to the GoPro itself, over Bluetooth
  • Starts and stops recording, switches modes
  • Shows the GoPro's battery, card and recording time
  • Changes the GoPro's settings (Pro)

Camera Remote facts are from Apple's Apple Watch User Guide.

Questions

What is Camera Remote on Apple Watch?

Apple's built-in app that turns the watch into a viewfinder and shutter for your iPhone's camera. Photos and videos save to the iPhone. Apple's guide.

Can Camera Remote control a GoPro?

No. It only works with the camera in your paired iPhone, so a GoPro never shows up in it.

Can Apple Watch control a GoPro at all?

Yes, with a GoPro app on the watch. GoPro doesn't make one any more, but third-party apps like Overcrank do. How to set it up.

Can I see the GoPro's view on my watch?

In Overcrank Pro, yes: a framing preview relayed by your iPhone, which has to be nearby. Record, modes and status work with the watch alone.
